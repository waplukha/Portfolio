A life between two worlds

Ram Krishna Bantawa is a Nepali author, novelist and lyricist. He has spent most of his life in Hong Kong, yet his writing keeps a deep connection to Nepali culture, traditions and society.

Born in Khotang District as the youngest son of Pancham and BhadraSara Bantawa, he published his first novel, Kharani, in 1988.

His 2008 novel Saghan Tuwanlo was translated into English as Shrill Mist by Nara Devi Rai, and Aamalai Chitthi (2019) is being translated by Dr. Devi Panthi. His 2005 album Aavaa brought stardom to singer Abha Mukarung.
